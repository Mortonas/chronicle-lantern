# Club prep contracts

Updated 2026-09-07. Whole-sheet interpretation is owned by `core/club_prep.py`; local tag grouping is owned by `core/club_tag_groups.py`, coordinated by `ClubGenerationService`. Prompts live in `templates/club_prep.j2`. Canonical index, relationship, identity, skeleton, and rumor pipelines remain independent.

## Tag grouping and compact browsing

The production service builds encounter membership locally before AI readings, using sheet hashtags (including nested tags) and YAML frontmatter `tags` lists/strings. Tags are NFKC/case normalized and deduplicated; generic record-type tags (`npc`, `npcs`, `character`, `characters`, `character`, `character`, `campaign`, `campaign`) are ignored. Complete single-line Obsidian wikilinks are excluded in full, and the destination/title suffix of a complete single-line Markdown link is excluded when its destination begins with `#` or `<#`; visible link labels and surrounding prose remain eligible for genuine hashtags. Incomplete, malformed, multiline, reference-link, and other unsupported link syntax remains ordinary prose under the existing hashtag scanner. Faction tags are eligible under the current tag-based grouping policy. No invented tags or inferred AI topics participate.

Starting from singletons, merge the pair of clusters with the strongest mean count of shared tags across their member pairs, only when the overlap is positive and the merged size is at most four. Exact ties prefer larger merged groups, then normalized member names and NPC IDs. This fills equally compatible groups without forcing unrelated guests together. Guests with no available matching group remain solo. Group numbering still uses member names/IDs, groups first and loners second, with the late arrival reserved last. Input order cannot change the result.

Tag arrangement replaces AI encounter selection in the production service, including provider-failure paths. It is recomputed cheaply from immutable sheets on retries and late-arrival changes, and contributes to the dependent prep identity. It is never labeled as AI success. AI receives fixed membership constraints for opening/notes; tag labels and reasons are excluded from those constraints and from NPC structural context. Shared tags do not establish friendship or mutual knowledge. The older row-admission helpers remain available for legacy/direct engine requests only.

The GM browser uses one numbered link per group, showing its tag-derived label and guest count. Hover previews member names; click opens member links and matching tags in the existing drawer without a provider request. Solo names and the late-arrival instruction remain visible. Copied prep retains all member names and the same numbering; normal JSON includes the group label. The clickable roster remains available.

Whole-sheet prep can add conversational presentation without changing this arrangement. Its `encounter_cues` response must contain every present encounter in canonical order and exactly echo each encounter number and ordered member-ID list; missing, duplicate, added, moved, reordered, or late-arrival rows reject the batch atomically. Groups receive a suggested activity, possible topic, short free-form social temperature, and player entry. A singleton may receive an optional approach, including a valid empty approach when no grounded suggestion is available. All fields are GM suggestions rather than current actions, relationships, compatibility findings, or shared knowledge.

Every group member needs substantive retained-passage support. A non-empty singleton approach needs support owned by that singleton. Evidence may name only encounter members. Public passages need no cross-member proof; every private or unknown passage used for a group requires the existing exact actor-owned knowledge passage and target-passage triple for every other member. The validator rejects unsupported facts, outside-member names, unknown keys, wrong types, overlong prose, and secret leakage. It never sorts, repairs, or rehydrates provider membership.

Validated cues attach only as presentation on copied encounter rows. Invalid or unavailable output leaves the deterministic membership and cue strings untouched, preserves other accepted prep sections, and keeps prep incomplete for Retry Incomplete Prep. A failed forced regeneration may retain a valid exact-input cached cue batch with the existing cache/failure metadata. Failed or fallback output is never published as successful AI.

The existing compact group row and hover names remain unchanged. A singleton keeps its direct NPC link and `Solo` label; when a grounded approach exists, an adjacent `Approach` link opens local details. The late arrival never receives that link. One pure cue formatter feeds the drawer, copied prep, and readable debug. Normal JSON adds only sanitized `conversation_cue` prose under the owning encounter row; passage IDs, evidence, knowledge proofs, retained source text, and internal cue-stage state remain absent. Opt-in debug may include only the existing allow-listed support IDs and bounded redacted excerpts. Cue prose, support, and tag explanations never enter NPC context.

`tests/test_club_tag_groups.py` covers 40 guests, the four-person cap, strongest matches, stable ordering, nested/frontmatter tags, provider failure, cache reuse, changed sheets, late arrivals, knowledge isolation, and real Qt hover/click/copy controls. Debug CLI tests use isolated `--cache-root` directories, preventing old temporary sheet paths from contaminating later runs. No live provider quality run is needed for deterministic tag membership; AI-authored prose quality remains separately unverified.

## Visible prep

The GM brief contains Start the Scene, Who Matters Here (up to five power-ranked guests), Social Groups and Loners, Useful to Know (up to four details), and the existing Rumors in Circulation. The clickable roster and rumor controls/browsing remain. The NPC panel replaces its visible relationship selection with up to three `who_matters` entries, labeled Possible interest when inferred. Its other canonical sections and outgoing `people_here` data retain their factual contracts.

Power is effective political authority, influence, resources, and ability to compel or protect. The validator preserves accepted AI ordering; hidden influence is labeled. Reading a sheet does not establish that it contains power evidence. Incomplete assessments are stated beside the list. Failed ranking shows at most five known-position examples alphabetically without claiming power order; the full roster remains available separately.

Every guest appears exactly once in the encounter table. Each proposed group member has source support and a participation reason. Friendship, accessible interests, social compatibility, and political approaches can justify grouping; faction or hostility alone cannot. There is no group quota. Groups precede loners, sorted by member names with NPC IDs breaking equal names. The late arrival is the final singleton, marked Not here yet—reroll until arrival. Omitted attendees receive singleton entries marked arrangement unavailable; deliberate solitary entries retain their proposed cue. Rendering and copying never renumber or regenerate.

## Evidence and knowledge

Event builds capture complete immutable sheet text after verifying the indexed revision. Document-order passages retain heading, source path, and character offsets. IDs derive from NPC ID, revision, and offsets. Full text remains in retained runtime context; provider reading chunks cover the entire document without silent truncation. Successful independent chunks accumulate annotations; their combined card is only a navigation aid, capped at 480 serialized characters. Failed chunks remain explicitly incomplete.

Both event and NPC reasoning first see all roster cards and request candidates/evidence; finalization receives original passages. Rumor guidance is narrower: explicit references and query retrieval are restricted to non-rumor passages owned by the proposal's present candidate IDs, so rumor-source, late-arrival, and non-candidate passages never enter its final request. Retrieval resolves explicit valid references first, then distinct query-term overlap, weighting topic/heading matches twice and body matches once. NPC ID and source position break ties. At most six queries of 160 characters are accepted. No relationship link or attendee-name mention is required for retrieval. Mandatory roster data precedes direct references and additional ranked evidence. Oversized mandatory input makes the operation unavailable.

Characters can use their own history, motives, and secrets. Other attendees' private or unknown information needs an explicit actor-owned knowledge passage paired with the specific target passage. Knowing one fact does not grant access to other secrets. Shared private goals alone cannot justify grouping. Structural validation checks ownership, references, classifications, membership, and visibility; semantic faithfulness still requires the live quality evaluation below. Suggested actions and interests do not change canonical relationships or rumors.

NPC reasoning receives a separate structural scene projection: opening participant IDs, numbered encounter membership, availability, and late-arrival identity. GM notes, power assessments, opening prose, encounter cues, and group explanations are excluded even when their citations are public. Other-character cards are marked `research_only`; they cannot establish the clicked character's knowledge. Relevance describes possible interest and future approaches, without asserting new current actions or moving guests.

NPC finalization reserves all explicit evidence requests and at least one substantive own passage. Own query matches take priority; otherwise motives, backstory, and knowledge passages precede generic document-order support. Up to two more own passages precede bulk candidate evidence. Heading-only passages are excluded, but headings with body text remain eligible. Missing or oversized mandatory support stops finalization; it never silently disappears. A one-person relevance request returns an empty complete result without inference calls.

## NPC conversation openings

On-demand NPC prep runs an independent `conversation_openings` proposal/finalization stage after canonical panel presentation and relevance. It receives only the clicked NPC ID, canonical display name, compact reading card, and reading coverage, followed by admitted original passages from that clicked sheet. It never receives roster cards, target sheets, relationships, scene prose or structure, tag/group material, the canonical panel skeleton, or portrayal basis. The existing Right Now, Play Them, Agenda Tonight, Who Matters Here, If Approached, Keep Guarded, and Pressure / Hook contracts remain unchanged; Conversation Openings renders between Who Matters Here and If Approached.

The result contains at most one `easy`, `meaningful`, and `dangerous` lane. Each lane has a short topic players could raise, a conditional response, a possible gain, a possible risk, and exact retained passage IDs. Easy requires public/observable clicked-owned support. Meaningful and Dangerous may use clicked-owned private or unknown support as GM prep without granting that information to anyone else. The validator admits only substantive annotated clicked passages; rejects unknown, duplicate, unread, other-character, scripted-dialogue, pronoun, unsupported-name, duplicate-lane/topic/presentation, extra-field, and length violations; and restores lane order plus evidence order from the application-owned sheet. One mixed passage may support distinct lanes. Sparse and empty results are valid; unsupported lanes are never filled from canonical panel cues, group cues, or Pressure / Hook.

One pure formatter feeds Qt, table export, and readable debug, including distinct complete-empty and unavailable messages. Normal JSON, UI, and table export include presentation but not evidence or `Why?`. Copy NPC Debug and `--include-debug` retain validated IDs and resolve the union of relevance and conversation support through `PrepContext.debug_support`; readable debug labels each retained ID `Why?`. These paths are pure retained-state projections and never read cache, call a provider, or expose full sheets/raw responses.

## Selected-rumor social guidance

After canonical rumor selection and whole-sheet readings, an independent event stage may produce zero or one social approach for each already selected rumor. Proposal input contains only the exact ordered rumor item ID, unchanged summary, source NPC ID, mentioned NPC IDs, ordered source IDs, compact roster readings, structural encounter numbers/member IDs/availability, and late-arrival ID. Finalization adds only admitted original passages. It never receives relationships, tag labels or explanations, scene prose, encounter cues, GM notes, NPC panels, conversation openings, or unselected rumor-pool entries.

Canonical rumor provenance and conversational support are separate. A source or mentioned NPC is topic context, never automatically a route, speaker, listener, believer, or spreader. Individual routes use one present proposal candidate and substantive non-rumor passages owned only by that attendee. Group routes must exactly echo one present multi-member encounter, use proposal candidates, and cite substantive non-rumor support owned by every member. Private or unknown cross-member support requires the existing actor-owned knowledge-passage and exact target-passage proof for every other member. Unknown, unavailable, duplicate, unselected, or out-of-order IDs; mismatched groups; rumor-only support; unsupported names; truth-confirming prose; unsupported actor claims; extra fields; and overlong text fail closed. Prose that substantially repeats the canonical rumor proposition must retain explicit rumor, claim, report, whisper, possibility, or directly scoped whether/if framing; an unrelated condition or inquiry does not authorize a later factual claim.

The provider supplies only why an approach may work, a natural opening, a possible gain, and a social risk. The application supplies canonical names and always renders the route as `Possible approach: ask…`; even evidence-backed routes remain suggestions. A valid empty list is complete and cacheable. Unavailable analysis adds no fallback prose and keeps Retry Incomplete Prep available. Guidance is stored beside, never inside, canonical rumor records and remains outside `scene_prep` revision and NPC scene context.

One pure formatter feeds the dashboard, copied table prep, normal visible JSON, and readable debug. Screen, copy, and normal JSON omit evidence and knowledge proofs. Full debug retains the validated rows and resolves their passage IDs through bounded `PrepContext.debug_support`. The rumor browser remains the canonical full pool and never displays generated guidance for unselected rumors.

## Limits, cache, and failures

| Operation | Estimated input budget | Maximum provider requests including retries |
|---|---:|---:|
| One reading chunk | 9,000 tokens | 2 |
| Event proposal and finalization | 9,000 per request | 4 |
| NPC relevance proposal and finalization | 9,000 per request | 4 |
| NPC conversation proposal and finalization | 9,000 per request | 4 |
| Selected-rumor guidance proposal and finalization | 9,000 per request | 4 |
| Existing NPC presentation | 4,500 tokens | 2 |
| Entire event pass | Per-request limits above | 64, reserving 4 for scene reasoning and another 4 when selected rumors make guidance applicable |

The existing length-based estimator is used with retry space reserved. Provider calls use `LlmProvider` and configured transport settings. Authentication stops further calls; three consecutive transport failures stop the current pass. Failed output is never cached as successful AI.

Canonical presentation and prep reasoning share authentication classification, including chained typed/status exceptions and bounded message fallbacks. Missing credentials and rejected credentials receive distinct sanitized metadata. An authentication failure stops the remainder of the current operation, including later supplemental stages and forced regeneration, while allowing exact-input validated cache hits. A later explicit operation can retry after configuration is corrected. Non-authentication failure in one independent supplemental stage does not invalidate another.

`prep_readings` keys include sheet revision, NPC identity/name, chunk identity, reading schema/prompt versions, and provider/model configuration fingerprint. `prep_sections` keys additionally include the complete roster, reading digests and coverage, event inputs, late arrival, and dashboard route versions. Fixed-group cues are a separately validated prep section whose identity also includes `CLUB_PREP_ENCOUNTER_CUE_SCHEMA_VERSION`; changing that owner does not invalidate readings, unrelated scene reasoning, or NPC reasoning. Each section is validated and cached independently. `prep_relevance` additionally includes clicked NPC, canonical outgoing relationships, accepted scene revision, and panel route versions. `prep_npc_conversation` includes only its v1 owner, reading schema/prompt versions, provider/model fingerprint, clicked ID/name/revision, and clicked reading content/coverage; unrelated roster, event, group, scene, relevance, and panel changes do not invalidate it. `prep_rumor_guidance` includes its v1 owner, reading schema/prompt, provider fingerprint, exact selected-rumor descriptors, reading digest/coverage, structural arrangement, and late arrival. It excludes the shared reasoning identity, scene prose/cues, relevance, NPC conversation, and panel output. Valid empty entries are cached, malformed entries rebuild, failures do not poison success, and forced failure may retain an exact-input valid cache with bounded metadata. Export is a pure retained-state projection; bounded redacted debug excerpts never include whole sheets or raw provider responses.

The canonical event, exact selected rumors, roster, and deterministic groups become visible before AI readings. That initial baseline is not actionable: NPC preparation and GM table export remain disabled until the matching generation token returns a final complete, partial, or fallback result. A baseline whose worker fails remains visible but gated until Retry settles it. Settled incomplete results unlock those actions with explicit incomplete status in the UI and copied prep. Readiness guards are scoped to the active event generation token; settled NPC results remain scoped by prep revision and request ID, so stale work cannot unlock or relabel another event. Retry Incomplete Prep reuses completed chunks and valid sections with the same seed and rumor selection; changed evidence regenerates dependent sections. Late-arrival selection runs in the background, reuses readings, and installs identity and arrangement together, with a coherent fallback if AI fails. The previous settled view remains actionable until replacement. Main-thread Qt slots reject superseded generation tokens, scene revisions, and NPC requests. Replacement clears obsolete NPC panels; an old worker cannot clear a newer request's tracking entry.

Immediate and cache-resumed retries receive compact immutable `accepted_sections` constraints. Retained source support is validated locally rather than automatically resending all original passages; requests retrieve source passages needed for new output. The constraint block and evidence packing are rebuilt after each response. Incomplete sections being repaired are not frozen as accepted constraints. New sections pass combined attendee, arrival, membership, and numbering checks before cache publication. Mandatory constraint overflow preserves accepted sections and leaves pending work unavailable within the existing request limit.

Version ownership: reading schema/prompt, reasoning, encounter-cue schema, NPC-conversation schema, and rumor-guidance schema versions are in `core/club_prep.py`, covered by `tools/check_schema_version_bump.py`; dashboard/panel schema, prompt, and request owners remain in `core/club_generation.py`. Rumor guidance uses `CLUB_PREP_RUMOR_GUIDANCE_SCHEMA_VERSION = "club_prep_rumor_guidance_v1"`; NPC conversation retains its independent v1 owner. Index, identity, skeleton, rumor materialization, encounter-cue, reasoning, panel, dashboard prompt, and dashboard request versions are unchanged.

Tag arrangement and cue presentation use shared reasoning v4, dashboard schema v19, prompt v17, request v8, and cue schema v1. Panel schema/prompt/request remain v11/v14/v7. Reading schema/prompt stay at v1, preserving valid reading caches. Dashboard v19 causes the intended one-time dashboard/event invalidation for the new visible field; later guidance-only changes use the dedicated guidance owner. The following row-admission details apply to legacy/direct engine inference, not production tag membership.

Social discovery explicitly searches for friendships, compatible conversation, and political approaches before assigning solitary guests, within the existing six-query and four-request event limits. When arranging encounters, one substantive social passage per present guest receives priority over bulk candidate evidence (explicitly requested evidence still comes first). Missing support remains unavailable analysis, not evidence that a guest prefers solitude.

Each member reason records `participation_basis`: `connection` uses accessible other-character evidence or an existing named social connection; `social` may instead use the actor's own social tendency, excluding motive/rumor-only justifications and passages also annotated motives or rumor. This explains participation without granting knowledge of another guest's private information. Cross-character references still pass the same knowledge validator. Compatibility and truthful prose require the separate live quality evaluation; no group quota is imposed.

Encounter admission is per row at the generation boundary; the direct section validator remains strict. Every row involved in a duplicate-membership conflict is rejected, while unrelated valid rows survive. Accepted partial rows are cached and supplied as immutable `retained_encounters` on immediate and cache-resumed retries; new rows cannot relocate those guests. Exact validated echoes are ignored. Invalid rows and omitted guests retain unavailable status and the retry action. Visible screen/copy output explicitly distinguishes missing arrangements from deliberate loners. Existing numbering, late-arrival, NPC structural context, and canonical rumor contracts remain unchanged.

No live provider quality run was performed for this implementation. The following six scenario evaluations remain pending separately scoped provider approval under `docs/CLUB_LIVE_SMOKE.md`:

| Scenario | Required live observation |
|---|---|
| Unlinked backstory relevance | A meaningful original backstory passage omitted from the card leads to an actionable interest in an unlinked guest. |
| Hidden knowledge | A private fact can inform GM notes without becoming another character's knowledge; explicitly known facts stay narrowly scoped. |
| Political grouping | Each participant has a credible, accessible reason to join an approach; no invented friendship or established history. |
| Incompatible guests | Hostility or faction membership alone does not force a group or contradictory scene actions. |
| Mostly loners | The model accepts a sparse arrangement with credible solitary cues and complete numbering. |
| Power ranking | Supported effective influence can outrank formal office; dramatic relevance and relationship counts do not determine rank. |

For each approved run, inspect the opening, every member reason, GM notes, and corresponding NPC panels against original support. Acceptance requires brief actionable prose, no invented established history, no unsupported secret knowledge, and no contradictions between panels. Record provider/model, exact approved sources, call count, cache disposition, and actual outcomes. Do not label fallback or cached mock output as successful live evaluation.
